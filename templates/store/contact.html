Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

Creating an account is easy! Click on the "Register" button in the top navigation, fill out the required information, and verify your email address. You'll be ready to start shopping or selling immediately.

We accept various payment methods including Paystack, bank transfers, debit/credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Verve), and mobile money. All payments are processed securely through our trusted payment partners.

Delivery times vary by location and vendor. Standard delivery is 3-7 business days, express delivery is 1-3 business days, and same-day delivery is available in Lagos. You'll receive tracking information once your order ships.

To become a vendor, click on "Become a Vendor" in the navigation menu, complete the registration form with your business details, and submit required documents for verification. Once approved, you can start listing your products.

We offer a 30-day return policy for most items. Products must be in original condition with tags attached. Defective items can be returned for free, while other returns may incur shipping costs. Contact our support team to initiate a return.
