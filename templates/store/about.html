Our Story

Founded in 2024, Vintage Marketplace was born from a vision to revolutionize e-commerce in Nigeria. We recognized the need for a platform that could bridge the gap between talented Nigerian entrepreneurs and customers seeking quality products.

Starting as a small team of passionate developers and business enthusiasts, we've grown into Nigeria's most trusted online marketplace. Our platform now serves thousands of vendors and millions of customers across all 36 states.

Today, we continue to innovate and expand, introducing new features like flash sales, loyalty programs, and advanced analytics to help our vendors succeed and provide customers with the best shopping experience possible.
